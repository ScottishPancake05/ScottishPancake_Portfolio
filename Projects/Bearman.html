Beardman Adventure

Beardman Adventure was my first 2D platformer shooter project.

During this project I learned the basics of creating gameplay systems, player movement, shooting mechanics, level design and working with Unity.

It was an important project that helped me understand the complete game development workflow.

Genre: Platformer Shooter

Platform: PC, Console

Status: Finished Demo

Engine: Unity
