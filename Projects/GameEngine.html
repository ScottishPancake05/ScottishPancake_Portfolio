Pancake Engine V1

Oh yeah, minigame time!

---
I created the Pancake Engine to get a basic understanding of how to use C++. To turn it into a challenge for myself, I originally wanted to make short minigames in the Visual Studio console.

I did some research and asked for advice from classmates who had worked with C++ before on how to get started. T hey recommended I download SFML (Simple and Fast Multimedia Library). With SFML, I could build a wide variety of 2D games, and it saved me from doing a lot of heavy math!

I followed a handful of tutorials to get the basics down and ended up making three short minigames. This actually turned into a school project, and I got full marks on it! (Yippee!)
