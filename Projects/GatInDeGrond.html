Gat In De Grond

Gat In De Grond is an educational AR prototype I developed during my internship at MaMa Producties. The goal was to create a fun, interactive experience that teaches players about the 11th and 12th century history of Woensel, specifically around the Sint-Petruskerk where historical skeletons were discovered! (Fun fact)

The experience starts with a virtual hole opening up in the ground. You walk up to it in real life until you are close enough to interact with a skeleton named Skelly! He tells you all about where he is from and shares the history of Eindhoven and Woensel from many years ago.

I dove into a collection of history books provided by MaMa Producties. I did all my own research on the local artifacts and skeletons that were excavated, which was incredibly interesting to learn about!
