Vice Noire

Vice Noire is a first person shooter, you play as a retired detective that could never let this one specific crime case go.

Many years later, the pieces of the puzzle suddenly clicks in your head and you decide to go after the criminal that haunted your prime days.

---
So a little fun fact about this game is that it was based on a short story I have written for my English exam. I was really proud of it and it even recieved a great score, it ended up in the short stories book from school at the Christmas market in 2024!

Genre: Shooter

Platform: PC

Status: Finished Demo

Engine: Unity
