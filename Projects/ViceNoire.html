Vice Noire

Vice Noire was my very first passion project that I started in my first year of game development.

The game was based on a story I wrote for an English exam during that same year. The story received a good mark and was even selected for the 2024 SintLucas student story book.

This project helped me learn more about Unity, game design, programming and creating a complete game experience.

Genre: Shooter

Platform: PC

Status: Finished Demo

Engine: Unity
