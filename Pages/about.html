Hi, I'm Joanne. Also known as ScottishPancake!

I am a passionate game developer with a love for every kind of video game! I’ve been a gamer my whole life and always dreamed of creating my own worlds one day.

My coding journey started in 2020 while studying IT, where I got to tinker with Raspberry PIs and dive into Python. That experience set my path in stone! In late 2022, I started learning C# and Unity, and I completely fell in love with it.

I have been making games ever since, constantly learning new things, and I am always looking for new challenges!
